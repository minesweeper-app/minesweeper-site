Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for iPhone, iPad & Mac players

Is Minesweeper free?
Check the App Store listing for current pricing. The app is a one-time purchase with no ads or in-app purchases.
Does Minesweeper work offline?
Yes. The game is 100% offline — no internet needed, no data usage. The only network feature is an optional 'More Apps' section in Settings that can be ignored.
Is my game data uploaded anywhere?
No. All settings and game data are stored locally on your device. The only network access is the optional 'More Apps' feature, which sends no personal data.
Which devices are supported?
Minesweeper is a universal app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac with native controls and Retina graphics. Requires iOS/iPadOS 15.0+ and macOS 15.0 (Sequoia)+. One purchase works everywhere.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. Minesweeper is completely ad-free with no pop-ups or in-app purchases. Just pure, focused gameplay.
