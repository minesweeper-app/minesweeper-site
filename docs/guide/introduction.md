# Introduction

## About Minesweeper

**Minesweeper — The Classic Game, Perfectly Made.**

![Minesweeper running on iPad](/screenshot-ipad-main.png)

Rediscover the timeless puzzle game you love, refined for modern devices with smooth controls, clean visuals, and endless replayability. Whether you are a first-time player or a seasoned pro, this is the Minesweeper experience you have been waiting for.

## Core Features

### 💣 **Authentic Gameplay**
True to the original rules — tap to reveal tiles, long-press to flag mines, and use logic to clear the board safely.

### 🎯 **3 Difficulty Levels**
Three preset difficulties with board sizes optimized per device:

| Level | iPhone | iPad | Mac |
|-------|--------|------|-----|
| Beginner | 10×14 (14 mines) | 16×16 (18 mines) | 9×9 (10 mines) |
| Intermediate | 14×22 (45 mines) | 18×16 (55 mines) | 16×16 (40 mines) |
| Expert | 16×30 (99 mines) | 18×24 (99 mines) | 30×16 (99 mines) |

### 📱 **Universal App**
One purchase works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Native controls for each platform — touch gestures on iOS, mouse and keyboard on Mac.

![Minesweeper on iPhone](/screenshot-iphone-main.png)

![Minesweeper on Mac](/screenshot-mac-difficulty.png)

### 🔇 **Clean & Ad-Free**
No interruptions, no pop-ups — just pure, focused gameplay.

### 🌍 **25 Languages**
Switch languages at runtime from Settings without restarting the app. Supports English, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Portuguese, Dutch, Vietnamese, Thai, Hindi, Turkish, Greek, Finnish, Norwegian, Danish, Hungarian, Indonesian, Malay, Ukrainian, and German (Switzerland).

### 🔊 **Sound Effects**
Subtle sound effects for clicks, flags, and game events — toggle on or off in Settings.

### ⌨️ **Keyboard Shortcuts (Mac)**
- `⌘N` — New game
- `⌘1` / `⌘2` / `⌘3` — Beginner / Intermediate / Expert
- `⌘⇧R` — Reveal mines (practice mode)

## System Requirements

- **iPhone/iPad**: iOS/iPadOS 15.0 or later
- **Mac**: macOS 15.0 (Sequoia) or later (Apple Silicon and Intel supported)
- **Storage**: ~50 MB

## Privacy

Minesweeper is offline-first. The game itself requires no network connection. No personal data is collected, no analytics, no tracking. The only network access is an optional "More Apps" section in Settings that fetches a list of sibling apps — this can be ignored and does not affect gameplay.

See the [Privacy Policy](/guide/privacy) for details.
